# Fresh computer science graduate review

This is a simulated beginner-perspective audit by the implementation agent, not feedback from an actual student or a controlled learner study.

**Can I begin without knowing monitoring terminology?** Yes. The first mission defines a measured value, a timestamp and a unit before introducing a time series. Counters and gauges precede rates. Observations and cumulative bucket rules precede p95. Programs, processes and services precede trace context. Each lesson gives a familiar analogy and one prediction.

**Can I understand what changed?** Every concept has narrated steps, pause/scrub controls, and a written alternative. Selected lessons expose numeric controls. The rate/irate, reset, over-time and bucket calculations have exact regression tests. The live engines are explicitly separated from illustrations so I know which numbers are measured.

**Can I copy an answer and call myself an expert?** Course material is open, including solutions. Assessments check a randomized five-question sample plus practical evidence in two scenarios, and cannot accept a client-reported score. This is helpful educational feedback, not a secure certification exam. The notebook and unfamiliar capstone are necessary opportunities to explain reasoning beyond copying.

**Does the tutor always know the answer?** No. The live smoke evaluation found an invented unbounded-histogram calculation and an overclaim about gauge persistence. Those findings led to stronger instructions, targeted misconception checks, and authored worked examples. Provider/schema/citation failures fall back to the course guide. Other AI errors remain possible; citations identify evidence, not a proof of correctness.

**Will the material stick?** Retrieval practice and spaced review are implemented. Correct review delays grow to 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days; a miss returns after ten minutes. Clicking repeatedly before a card is due does not increase its spaced repetition count. Retention gains still need to be measured with real learners.

**What does “hero” mean here?** A successful learner can explain metric types, read and write the supported PromQL patterns, distinguish SQL analogies, trace a request, inspect SDK evidence and reason about the supplied incidents. This course does not establish expertise in multi-cluster operations, large production datasets, advanced Collector deployment, Grafana administration or every native-histogram feature. Those are next-stage projects.

**Release judgment:** Suitable as an accessible guided practice product, with a transparent scope and tested exercises. Validate the path with five absolute beginners before advertising measured learning or job-readiness outcomes. Ask them to explain a bucket, predict a reset, debug a broken trace, and solve the independent capstone without the answer key. Track where they need help and repeat the same conceptual checks a week later.
